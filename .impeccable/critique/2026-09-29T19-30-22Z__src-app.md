## Design Specificity Verdict
- The moments are authored: the board (glyphs and pips, not colour alone), buoys landing on the chosen corner, the Passage stamp, days at sea and the replay strip.
- The frames are generic: home rows with chevrons, Port Math as cards plus buttons 1–6, Hand Tracker as a log card plus a 20-key pad.
- Pip Flash is the only format that stays on the island, which is likely why it is the one players love.
- Detector: 4 findings, all design-system drift, and all false positives. Three are Satori OG font aliases (ArchivoWide and ArchivoItalic); the fourth is the dusk themeColor literal in layout.tsx.
- Browser overlay: clean on /, Pip Flash, Port Math and /c/unknown. On the result page the detector flagged flat-type-hierarchy, partly real: "The passage" h2 is body size.
- Objective checks: no overflow in either theme, no tap targets under 44px, and ink-2 and accent contrast of 5.5–7.7:1 in both palettes.

## Priority Issues
1. **[P1] The home board shows today's Pip Flash Daily board 1 untimed** (TodayStrip IslandPlate, and the OG card too). Players can study it before the clock starts, which breaks the fair-scoring promise. Fix: give the home and OG island their own seed, or show the Daily's board only after it has been played. Command: /impeccable harden
2. **[P1] Hand Tracker is hard for the wrong reasons.** The target resource is hidden, only one line is visible at a time, and the pad has 20 keys. Fix: on easy and medium, show "Tracking: brick" from the start; stack the last lines, fading the older ones; keep the hidden target for hard only. Command: /impeccable clarify
3. **[P1] Port Math still feels like a spreadsheet.** It has no island, no pressure, and the rate chips are all one blue. Fix: show the ports as resource-coloured chips (the board's language) and consider a gentle, visible pace. Command: /impeccable bolder
4. **[P2] The play-screen chrome eats about 35% of the height above the question.** Fix: hide the app header during a run (the X already exits) and fold "Rush · 1 of 13" and the tier into the dots row. Command: /impeccable distill
5. **[P2] The home repeats itself.** Each format appears twice (in the Dailies list and in the drills list), the CTA duplicates the first row, and link labels are inconsistent. Fix: one row per format with its Daily status and a Rush action, plus a single CTA. Command: /impeccable distill
